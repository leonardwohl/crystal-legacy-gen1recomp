# Legacy: Pokédex

Crystal Legacy's extra Pokédex pages (after Nayru62's Pokédex): press SELECT on an entry for Stats, with Moves, Evolution and Area pages to follow.

## What it changes

- Crystal Legacy's extra Pokédex pages, after Nayru62's Pokédex (see CREDITS.md)
- On a Pokédex entry, press **SELECT** for the extra pages; **SELECT** or **◀▶** moves between them and **B** goes back to the entry
- **Stats:** types, base stats and their total, catch rate, base exp, growth rate, egg groups, gender ratio and hatch steps
- Coming next: Moves, Evolution and Area pages
- Shows whatever data is loaded: with Legacy: Pokémon Changes installed, Crystal Legacy's stats; otherwise vanilla's

Games: crystal. Needs gen1recomp >=0.4.0 <0.5.0.
