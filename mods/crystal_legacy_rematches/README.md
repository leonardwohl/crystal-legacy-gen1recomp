# Legacy: Rematches

Rematch every gym leader after the Elite Four, and the Elite Four themselves once you have all 16 badges.

## What it changes

- Gym leader rematches after the Elite Four: Falkner, Bugsy, Whitney, Morty, Pryce, Jasmine, Chuck, Clair
- Elite Four and Lance rematch teams once you have all 16 badges
- Crystal Legacy's rematch dialogue and teams (levels 53-69)
- Red's rematch is part of Story Changes (it follows Crystal Legacy's Mewtwo event)

Games: crystal. Needs gen1recomp >=0.4.0 <0.5.0.
