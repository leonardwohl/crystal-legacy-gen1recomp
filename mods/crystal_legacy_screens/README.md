# Legacy: Screens

Crystal Legacy's extra stats page (where and when a Pokémon was met, its DVs and Hidden Power), happiness on the stats screen, and its trainer card: coloured badges and a real Kanto page.

## What it changes

- **Stats screen, page 4** (orange, after Nayru62's Ultimate stats pages): where, when and at what level the Pokémon was met, and its DVs. Once you've caught every Unown, its Hidden Power type and power too, written in the Unown alphabet as in Crystal Legacy
- **Happiness** on the blue page
- Toggle: **STATS PAGE 4** (OPTIONS -> CRYSTAL LEGACY, or the MODS menu)
- **Trainer card**: every badge in its own colours and each leader's face in their colours; page 3 becomes a real Kanto page, with Kanto's leaders and the Kanto badges you hold (vanilla redraws Johto's). A on page 2 goes on to it. Toggle: **TRAINER CARD**
- The leader faces and badge pictures are Crystal Legacy's art. Copy `johto_leaders.png`, `johto_badges.png`, `kanto_leaders.png` and `kanto_badges.png` from Crystal Legacy's `gfx/trainer_card` into this mod's folder as `card/`. Without them the badges are still coloured, and page 3 stays as in vanilla

Games: crystal. Needs gen1recomp >=0.4.0 <0.5.0.
