# Legacy: Quality of Life

Crystal Legacy's quality-of-life touches: run with B, reuse Repels, sort the PACK with START, a quicker nurse, and a Day-Care that keeps your Pokémon's experience.

## What it changes

- Hold B to run (walking at bike speed), and to surf faster. The player wears Crystal Legacy's running sprite while running
- When a Repel wears off and you have another of the same kind, you're asked "Use another?"
- START in the PACK sorts your items into Crystal Legacy's order
- After your first visit, the Pokémon Center nurse skips her speech and heals straight away
- The Day-Care keeps the experience your Pokémon earned (vanilla throws away everything past its current level's minimum)
- Each change can be switched off in the mod's options

Games: crystal. Needs gen1recomp >=0.4.0 <0.5.0.
