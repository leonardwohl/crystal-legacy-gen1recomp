# Credits

These mods port the work of **[Pokémon Crystal Legacy](https://github.com/cRz-Shadows/Pokemon_Crystal_Legacy)**
to [gen1recomp](https://github.com/bryanthaboi/gen1recomp). All of the design —
the balance, the story, the teams, the features — is theirs. The credits below
are carried over from Crystal Legacy's own README, so everyone they thank is
thanked here too.

## Pokédex

The Legacy: Pokédex mod follows Crystal Legacy's Pokédex, which builds on:

- **Nayru62** — [Nayru's Pokédex](https://github.com/Nayru62/pokecrystal/tree/Nayrus-Pokedex-Vanilla-TypeGFX-BETA)
  and the [Ultimate Pokémon stats pages](https://github.com/Nayru62/pokecrystal/tree/stats-pages-utilities-vanilla);
  Crystal Legacy also thanks Nayru62 for their help with the Pokédex and Pokémon pages
- **Idain** — [Option to show shiny colors in Pokédex](https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal/wiki/Option-to-show-shiny-colors-in-Pok%C3%A9dex)
- **NobodySociety** — [Customizable Pokédex Color](https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal/wiki/Customizable-Pok%C3%A9dex-Color)
- **Nick-PC** — [Splash a Pokédex Entry from an Overworld Event (Generation I)](https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal/wiki/Splash-a-Pok%C3%A9dex-Entry-from-an-Overworld-Event-(Generation-I))

## Code credits (Crystal Legacy)

- **Rangi42** — pret wiki tutorials: new maps and landmarks, trainer classes,
